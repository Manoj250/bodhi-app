BODHI

Privacy policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

Bodhi helps a group ride together: a shared map, stops, chat, ride voice and SOS. We keep only what that needs, we never sell your data, and we never show ads.

What we keep about you

  • Your sign-in. When you sign in with Google we receive your email address, the name on your Google account and a link to your Google profile photo. You can replace the photo in the app.
  • Phone numbers of people you choose. When you save a friend's number from your contacts, we store that one number with your friendship. Only you can see it. We never read or upload the rest of your contacts.
  • Your rides. Ride names, planned stops, who rode, stop requests, and the ride's text chat. If you meet the crew at a later stop, we keep where you'll meet them and any stops of your own on the way; only you see your own stops.
  • Your SOS PIN, stored only as a one-way hash so nobody can read it.

Location

Bodhi uses your location only during an active ride. Your live position goes to your crew and to the guardians you picked, and we hold only the latest one while you ride. To draw your ride recap we keep a coarse trace (about one point every 30 seconds) for each ride. When you're not on a ride, Bodhi doesn't track you.

While a ride is live, Android shows a "Ride in progress" notification: that's Bodhi sharing your location with your crew in the background. Ending the ride stops it.

Safety features

If you send an SOS (or Auto-SOS sends one after a crash), we share your location with your crew and guardians and keep a record of the SOS. Auto-SOS uses your phone's motion sensor during a ride; that data stays on your phone.

While your SOS is on, your phone also records audio around you in short clips and uploads them, so your crew and guardians can hear what's happening. Those recordings may include other people's voices. They're kept with the SOS record and shared only with the people on that ride and your guardians. You can turn SOS audio off in Settings.

Ride voice

The ride voice channel streams your microphone to your crew only while your mic is switched on. It passes through our server live and is never recorded or stored. The only audio Bodhi ever keeps is the SOS clips described above. To connect you only when it's needed, the crew can see whose mic is on.

Who else sees your data

  • The people on your ride and the guardians you choose. Nobody else.
  • Services that run Bodhi for us: Google Cloud (our servers), Google Maps (maps, places and directions) and Firebase (notifications). They process data only to provide that service.

Google account data

Bodhi uses your Google account only to sign you in, with your name, email address and profile photo. We don't use it for anything else, we don't share it except as described above, and Bodhi's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

App usage

We record simple app events (for example "ride created") to see what works and fix what doesn't. They aren't used for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and rides while you use Bodhi. Ask us to delete your account and we'll delete it, your rides, tracks, chat and SOS records within 30 days.

Your choices

  • Remove a saved phone number or a guardian at any time in the app.
  • Turn Auto-SOS or SOS audio off in Settings.
  • Turn off location or microphone access in Android settings at any time.
  • To get a copy of your data or delete your account, email bodhi.rides.help@gmail.com.

Age

Bodhi is for people 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger; if you think someone under 18 has signed up, email us and we'll delete the account.

Security

Data travels to our servers encrypted (HTTPS), sign-in tokens are kept in your phone's secure storage, and your SOS PIN is never stored in readable form.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date, and tell you in the app if the change matters to how your data is used.
