Bodhi

Stay close on the open road. Share your ride, your vibe, and never lose your people.

Android app · coming to Google Play
  • Plan the ride together Pick stops from Google Maps, invite your crew with one link, and meet up at a later stop if you're joining partway.
  • One live map for the crew See everyone on the road, with the route and turns, and a camera that follows you.
  • Stops and detours Ask the crew to pull over, or ride to a crewmate who needs you.
  • Ride voice An open-mic channel for the crew that works with your helmet headset. Streamed live, never recorded.
  • SOS when it matters Hold to send, PIN to cancel. Your crew and the guardians you choose get your location right away.
  • A recap of every ride Distance, time, stops and the crew, ready to share.

Bodhi uses your location only during an active ride. Read how in our privacy policy.
